ACME CORP — INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY
Last updated: April 2026

1. Device Requirements
All devices used to access company systems, whether company-issued or
personal (BYOD), must have full-disk encryption enabled, an up-to-date
operating system, and endpoint protection software installed by IT.

2. VPN Usage
Company VPN is mandatory for accessing internal tools, source code
repositories, and customer data from any network outside the office,
including home networks used for remote work.

3. Password and MFA
All employee accounts require multi-factor authentication (MFA). Passwords
must be at least 14 characters and rotated every 180 days.

4. Data Classification
Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted.
Confidential and Restricted data may not be stored on personal devices or
in unsanctioned cloud storage (e.g., personal Google Drive or Dropbox).

5. Incident Reporting
Any suspected security incident, including lost devices or phishing
attempts, must be reported to security@acme.example within 1 hour of
discovery.

6. Travel Security
Employees traveling internationally with company devices should enable
travel mode on MFA tokens and avoid connecting to untrusted public Wi-Fi
without the VPN active.
