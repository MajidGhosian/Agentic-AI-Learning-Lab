ACME CORP — REMOTE WORK POLICY
Last updated: March 2026

1. Eligibility
All full-time employees who have completed their 90-day probationary period are
eligible to work remotely up to 3 days per week. Managers may approve fully
remote arrangements on a case-by-case basis, subject to VP approval.

2. Equipment
Acme provides a one-time home office stipend of $500 for remote employees to
purchase a monitor, chair, or desk. Laptops and standard peripherals are
provided by IT and remain company property.

3. Core Hours
Remote employees must be reachable on Slack and email between 10:00 AM and
3:00 PM in their local time zone, regardless of which days they work remotely.

4. Security Requirements
Remote work requires the use of the company VPN for all access to internal
systems, full-disk encryption on any device used for work, and a locking
screen after 5 minutes of inactivity. See the Security Policy document for
full details.

5. International Remote Work
Employees wishing to work remotely from outside their home country for more
than 14 consecutive days must file a request with HR at least 30 days in
advance, due to tax and legal compliance requirements.
