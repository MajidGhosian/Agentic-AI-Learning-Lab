ACME CORP — EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY
Last updated: January 2026

1. Eligible Expenses
Employees may be reimbursed for reasonable business expenses including
travel, client meals, conference registration fees, and approved software
subscriptions. Personal entertainment, alcohol at non-client meals, and
first-class airfare are not reimbursable.

2. Submission Process
All expense reports must be submitted through the Expensify portal within
30 days of the expense being incurred. Reports submitted after 60 days will
not be reimbursed except in extraordinary circumstances approved by Finance.

3. Receipts
Original itemized receipts are required for any single expense over $25.
Credit card statements alone are not sufficient documentation.

4. Home Office Stipend
As referenced in the Remote Work Policy, a one-time $500 home office stipend
is available to eligible remote employees and should be submitted as a
"Home Office" expense category with proof of purchase.

5. Approval Thresholds
Expenses under $200 are auto-approved upon manager sign-off. Expenses between
$200 and $2,000 require manager and Finance approval. Expenses over $2,000
require VP approval in addition to Finance.

6. Reimbursement Timeline
Approved reimbursements are paid out with the next standard payroll cycle,
typically within 10 business days of approval.
